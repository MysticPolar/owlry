01 · Problem

Readers don’t need another shelf.
They need the right book for tonight.

Discovery is joyless

Feeds and star ratings optimize for popularity, not fit for this mood. BookTok moves culture — then dumps you into a wishlist.

Commitment is expensive

A book is hours of life. Readers can’t cheaply tell whether it’s for them right now — so they bounce, or never start.

The habit is fragile

Without a warm reason to return daily, reading falls off the calendar. Catalog apps don’t create a loop; they archive guilt.

Goodreads catalogs the past. StoryGraph charts it. Fable socializes it. Nobody owns “what should I read tonight?”

02 · Solution

Correspondence, not catalog.

Owlry reframes discovery as mail from the owl post office. You ask; Scout sorts; Peek arrives as a letter you can taste — then reading refills the well.

Ask

Tell Scout what’s going on — a mood, a problem, a rainy Sunday. Two desks: everything, or non-fiction office hours.

Peek

Open a grounded look inside a real book: the right chapter, core idea, three insights — taste before you commit.

Grow

XP, ink, streaks, and Mirror’s reading identity. The loop feeds itself: reading pages refill the inkwell.

04 · Why now

Mood discovery is the job BookTok promised.

Cultural

Gen Z reads by moment and creator cue — then hits a dead end in catalogs. Creators need a product that demos in nine seconds: type a mood, a peek arrives.

Technical

LLMs finally make high-quality, book-grounded peeks and conversational curation cheap enough to meter — if you own the loop and the cost floor.

Competitive

Incumbents still shelf by genre. ChatGPT can recommend a title; it doesn’t build a reading habit, a cast, or a peek you return to finish.

Window

Platform volatility pushed BookTok attention elsewhere. A warm, ownable home for mood reading is a closing window — not a forever option.

07 · Unit economics · design principles

Subscription is the instrument. Margin is the test.

Cost floor

Every live Scout turn and generated Peek has a known LLM COGS. Ink pricing and monthly allotments are set so contribution margin per active stays positive at target usage.

Offline-safe product

Classic mockup brain + curated peeks keep the desk useful when live AI is off — protecting experience and COGS during spikes.

08 · Go-to-market

Two channels. One ownable asset.

Micro-creators · 5–50k

BookTok / Reels. Product demos in nine seconds. Bespoke peek per creator so the video is about their moment — not a generic AI ad.

The Owl Post newsletter

Weekly: one mood, five books, one letter. A product demo we own — mood-SEO pages (“books for when you can’t sleep”).

Share cards

Radar portraits & peek excerpts. Screenshots are the k-factor — export before we pretend we have a social graph.

Indie affiliate

Bookshop.org (and similar) on about sheets — day-one, zero CAC commerce halo. Contemporary titles bought there; classics readable in-app.
