What v4.2 merges

  1. From the prompt package: output schemas for all four roles, runner-computed scores from 35 ratings, exact-edit and protected-span contracts, finding statuses (observed, suspected, unverified), no_change / escalate actions, question-weighted reporting, Set B reuse limit.
  2. From the runner package: two samples per question with the sequential gate, re-baseline on promote, deterministic form metrics and topicality lint, ablation iterations, the ledger as a binding record, lane profiles, the dispatcher for subagent-only models.
  3. New: both lineages in one batch, one evaluator and judge for both, and the capability report with capability-bound cells feeding the optimizer as numbers only.
  4. Rubric: the owner's matrix verbatim; a minor weakness with the ★ met may reach 9.49, because the band text defines 9–10 as no material weakness. Suspected misattribution rates C2.3 but never blocks: there is no evidence adapter.
  5. Champion selection by the gate: v0 is the original prompt; the round-12 v2 enters as the first manual challenger; whichever wins is the champion.
